World Shaper

Shape the battlefield. Break the terrain. Defeat the boss!

World Shaper is a single-player 2D action platformer focused on boss battles and terrain manipulation. You play as a warrior who has the power to create and destroy terrain during combat. Create floating platforms to move through the battlefield, build stone walls to block attacks, and summon falling stone spikes to damage enemies. You can also use ranged attacks and melee attacks to destroy terrain and fight the bosses. Each boss has multiple phases with different attacks and abilities. As the battle progresses, the boss becomes more aggressive and may move or destroy the terrain around you. Use your movement, attacks and terrain-creation abilities to survive each battle and defeat every boss!
